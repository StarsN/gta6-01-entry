**Palmera Heat** — a top-down open-world crime game that plays in a desktop browser from static files, no network needed. Explore the fictional sun-soaked coastal city of Palmera on foot and by car: steal vehicles, weave through traffic and pedestrians, take on a chain of story missions, fight with fists and a pistol, and outrun a police force that answers a five-star wanted level. It has a full HUD with a live minimap, a start screen with the story so far, pause, and a save you can continue. Every name, character, street and sound is original to this game, and all of its sound is synthesized live with WebAudio — there are no asset files to load.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move and drive, E to enter or leave a car, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, click or Space to fight and shoot, P or Esc to pause, M to mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```
